respectively. The friction and windage losses are 20 kW, and its core losses are 12 kW.  
The armature winding is distributed in 60 slots with 4 cond/slot. The number of turns 
of the field winding is 100. The O.C.C. at rated frequency is as follows: 
MMF 
2. The saturated and unsaturated synchronous reactance using two methods.  
3. The field current, open circuit voltage and voltage regulation at different load 
conditions using both the exact and approximate methods. 
4. The output active, reactive and apparent power at each load condition.  
5. The prime-mover power and torque at each load condition. 
Exercise your program on full load and half load case: 0.8 lagging pf, 0.9 lagging pf,
